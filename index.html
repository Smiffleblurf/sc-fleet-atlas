Reading the matrix: Each ship is placed in its primary professional role and an approximate operating-crew tier (not an official minimum crew specification). Ground vehicles are separated into their own column. Toggle secondary capabilities for hybrid ships. These classifications are community-editable planning estimates, not verified CIG minimum or maximum crew numbers. It does not automatically learn newly released ships.
Accuracy & privacy. Ship names and quantities are provided by each visitor through a HangarXPLOR-style JSON. Profession tags and crew tiers are editorial planning assignments, not official current ship specifications; component availability, concept status and gameplay systems may change. This public app starts empty and includes no individual fleet, account IDs, pledge prices or account history. Imported files remain on your device—no upload service or network requests are used. The built-in classification catalog is manually maintained and does not automatically sync with CIG or any third-party API. Unknown ships need manual review. Official career framework: RSI / CIG ship careers. Community ship data: The Shipyard: Careers & Roles.
