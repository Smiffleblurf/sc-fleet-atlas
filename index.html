Reading the matrix: Each ship is placed in its primary professional role and an approximate operating-crew tier (not an official minimum crew specification). Ground vehicles are separated into their own column. Greyed-out ship names are catalog references not found in your imported fleet; turn them off with the toggle. They are not counted as owned or included in gap calculations. Toggle secondary capabilities for hybrid ships. These classifications are community-editable planning estimates, not verified CIG minimum or maximum crew numbers. The public reference checks for new ships and vehicles online when available; new or ambiguous professions may require community review.
Accuracy & privacy. Ship names and quantities are provided by each visitor through a HangarXPLOR-style JSON. Profession tags and crew tiers are editorial planning assignments, not official current ship specifications; component availability, concept status and gameplay systems may change. This public app starts empty and includes no individual fleet, account IDs, pledge prices or account history. Imported files remain on your device and are never transmitted. The browser requests only public ship data from the community Star Citizen Wiki API (or a secondary public reference when unavailable); no account data is included in those requests. New ships appear when the upstream source adds them and the browser can reach it. The local catalog can lag official announcements; unmatched professions are shown as Needs classification. Unknown ships need manual review. Official career framework: RSI / CIG ship careers. Public vehicle data: Star Citizen Wiki API (primary); RSI Ship Matrix community snapshot (fallback).
