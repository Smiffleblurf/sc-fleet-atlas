New here? How to get your fleet JSON
  1. Install HangarXPLOR for Chrome, Firefox, or Opera.
  2. Sign in to RSI and open My Hangar. Wait for HangarXPLOR to finish loading your hangar.
  3. In HangarXPLOR, export your ships in Hangar Transfer Format (JSON). Save the downloaded JSON file; you do not need the CSV export.
  4. Return here, click Import fleet JSON, and select that file.

Browser compatibility: Chrome is not required. HangarXPLOR offers Chrome, Firefox, and Opera extensions; Fleet Atlas itself works in modern browsers. Your JSON is read locally, never uploaded.

Reading the matrix: Each ship is placed in its primary professional role and an approximate operating-crew tier (not an official minimum crew specification). Ground vehicles are separated into their own column. Greyed-out ship names are catalog references not found in your imported fleet; turn them off with the toggle. They are not counted as owned or included in gap calculations. Secondary capabilities are built-in; module roles require a specific configuration and appear as dotted chips. Amber dotted chips indicate planned modules. A hull alone does not guarantee access to a module, so module roles do not count as confirmed coverage. These are editorial planning estimates, not official crew specifications or gameplay guarantees. New or ambiguous professions may require review.
Accuracy & privacy. Ship names and quantities are provided by each visitor through a HangarXPLOR-style JSON. Profession tags and crew tiers are editorial planning assignments, not official current ship specifications; component availability, concept status and gameplay systems may change. This public app starts empty and includes no individual fleet, account IDs, pledge prices or account history. Imported files remain on your device and are never transmitted. The browser requests only public ship data from the community Star Citizen Wiki API (or a secondary public reference when unavailable); no account data is included in those requests. New ships appear when the upstream source adds them and the browser can reach it. The local catalog can lag official announcements; unmatched professions are shown as Needs classification. Unknown ships need manual review. Official career framework: RSI / CIG ship careers. Public vehicle data: Star Citizen Wiki API (primary); RSI Ship Matrix community snapshot (fallback). Community ship career reference: The Shipyard: Careers & Roles.
